One phase-continuous logarithmic chirp from 500 Hz to 8 kHz drives the exciter at the selected output level. The complete return is captured once, analysed from overlapping local windows with the learned input noise subtracted, refined around the strongest candidate and fitted in log-frequency to locate the resonance. Bode magnitude

Five shaped low-level pulses drive the exciter. Each return is located at its strongest excursion, normalised into a 30 ms view and its sign is voted. The result is the pickup's relative electrical phase against this fixture and wiring — compare pickups measured on the same setup.

Pulse response

Band-limited white noise drives the exciter for the chosen time. Several 8192-point Hann FFT frames are averaged into the measured spectrum, evenly spaced 4096-point frames build the spectral waterfall, and 16 spectral and time-domain metrics are derived. Broadband excitation makes Fundamental, THD and Even / odd comparative screening indicators rather than calibrated measurements.
