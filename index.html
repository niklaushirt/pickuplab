1
Build a repeatable magnetic driverFix a small coil above the pickup at a recorded height and orientation. Add current limiting appropriate to your interface or headphone amp. Never short an output.
2
Define positiveMark driver-coil winding direction, its face toward the pickup, cable polarity, pickup magnet face, and which pickup lead goes to input hot. Polarity results are relative to this convention.
3
Set clean gainUse Hi-Z input, disable pads/DSP/AGC, keep the interface below clipping, and do a low-level sweep first. A saturating preamp can masquerade as pickup saturation.
4
Validate with loopbackRun the saturation test through an attenuated electrical loopback. It should remain symmetric and linear. Any loopback compression/asymmetry is in the interface, not the pickup.
5
Control the loadRecord cable capacitance, volume/tone pot values, and interface input impedance. These strongly move the pickup resonance and must stay fixed for comparisons.
